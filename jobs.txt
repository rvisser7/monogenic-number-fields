# Jobs for scripts/run_jobs.py: one per line, a script in scripts/ and its arguments.
#     sage -python scripts/run_jobs.py jobs.txt --parallel 4
#
# PARI brute-force search (--method pari) on the fields with no row yet, with a bound -B
# that gets smaller as the degree goes up. The search tries up to about (2B+1)^(n-1)/2
# elements per field, all of them when the field has no generator up to height B. Time
# per such field, measured on one field of each degree:
#   degree 5:  B=3 0.02 s, B=4 0.05 s, B=5 0.12 s
#   degree 6:  B=2 0.04 s, B=3 0.20 s, B=4 0.66 s
#   degree 7:  B=2 0.22 s, B=3 1.9 s
#   degree 8:  B=1 0.08 s, B=2 2.0 s
#   degree 9:  B=1 0.20 s, B=2 13 s
#   degree 10: B=1 0.79 s, B=2 81 s
#   degree 12: B=1 11 s
# Fields where a generator is found at a lower height stop there, so most are faster.
# When the search finds nothing, the local tests (--method local) are tried as well.
#find_generators.py --degrees 5 --method pari -B 4 --only-new
